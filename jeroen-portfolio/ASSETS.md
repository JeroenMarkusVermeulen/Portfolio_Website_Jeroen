- `assets/images/quietcare-home-coverpage.png` and the architecture/workflow/privacy slides: original QuietCare case-study visuals.

- `assets/projects/pit-analysis/results.jpg`: the original additional project visual.

The profile initials and small interface icons use text and inline SVG. No stock portrait or new generated imagery was introduced.

The prior cinematic scene, vendored Three.js modules and generated cloud/tree assets now live in `../review/cinematic-original/`. Their historical asset documentation and vendor license are retained there. They are not included in the deployable site or requested by the current page.
